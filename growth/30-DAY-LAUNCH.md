# WCT 30-day visual-first discovery launch
October 28, 2026 baseline. Prepared for manual scheduling; nothing is auto-posted.

## Campaign premise
Use visible phenomena to earn attention, then link the same claim to an explanatory article and its exact proof or experiment. Never present a simulation as physical footage, a mathematical proof as experimental validation, or a candidate residual as discovery.

## Publish cadence
- 12 short wave/simulation edits (variants from original sources, not 12 new experiments)
- 4 measurement clips or figure explainers
- 4 math/Lean clips
- 2 full YouTube explainers
- 2 Substack features
- 20 short-form promotional posts from SOCIAL-POSTS.md

## Creative rules
Start each video with the motion or physical apparatus; no logo-only opening. Use 9:16 framing for shorts, readable 2-line overlays, meaningful captions, and captions for sound-off viewing. Label animated research visuals as numerical visualizations. Link to /discover/ or the exact source. Never use broad novelty or security claims beyond the source. Record the source asset, script, date and landing URL for every post.
