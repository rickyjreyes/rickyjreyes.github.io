A searchable record of named constructions, definitions, and technical terminology in Richard J. Reyes’s Wave Confinement Theory and Resonance-Confinement Architecture research. Each source-linked term connects to a dated publication and its supporting record where available.

First publication, exact-phrase search results, mathematical originality, scientific verification, and external adoption are different questions. This registry distinguishes them instead of treating any one as proof of the others. An explicit coinage claim means a term was asserted by Reyes as his terminology; a WCT-defined term is a model-specific usage rather than a claim of exclusive lexical ownership.

Attribution boundaries

Exactly what is claimed as Reyes's work?

Author: Richard J. Reyes. Research programs: Wave Confinement Theory (WCT), Resonance-Confinement Architecture (RCA), and related public studies. The glossary is an authored research index, not a claim that all listed scientific words, prior theories, formulas, or physical phenomena were invented here.

Tier 1 · Five explicitly asserted terminology coinages

These five phrases are explicitly claimed as coined by Reyes in the published RCA terminology audit. Their source is the June 11, 2025 RCA record (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.17732661). They survived a bounded indexed exact-phrase search; that result is not proof of universal historical first use.

  1. Coherence mirage — Polished or coherent-looking recursive AI output can mask degraded semantic grounding.
  2. Confinement Termination Principle — A proposed RCA rule for stopping unsafe symbolic evolution when confinement constraints fail.
  3. Physical Symbolic Kill Logic — A proposed substrate-level symbolic termination gate tied to coherence or confinement bounds.
  4. Propagation–correction criticality — A proposed stability boundary comparing recursive propagation against available correction.
  5. Semantic anchor decay — Deterioration of retained semantic reference points across recursive processing.

Tier 2 · 304 additional WCT-defined terms in the 22-release terminology audit

These are names, model-specific constructions, diagnostics, or definitions that Reyes formulated or used in the cited research publications. The claim is documented authorship of that particular formulation or specialization—not ownership of established mathematics, a universal originality finding, or independent validation of the physics.
