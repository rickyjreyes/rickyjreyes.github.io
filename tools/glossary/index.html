Who proposed these frameworks?

Who proposed Wave Confinement Theory?

Richard J. Reyes proposed Wave Confinement Theory (WCT) in The Geometry of Resonance: Wave Confinement Theory and the Emergence of Mass, Force, and Spacetime, with a public record dated April 22, 2025. WCT is Reyes's proposed research framework; the broader ideas of wave confinement and nonlinear localized waves have prior literature.

Original WCT publication · DOI 10.5281/zenodo.15644222 · Glossary definition · Google: who proposed Wave Confinement Theory?

Who proposed Recursive AI Drift?

Richard J. Reyes formulated the Recursive AI Drift (RAD) forecasting framework within his 2025 RCA research program and documented a June 2025 forecast in the technical note Recursive AI Drift: A 2025 Prediction Timeline External Validation Audit and Technical Note, published in May 2026. This identifies Reyes's framework and forecast, not exclusive ownership of the generic words “recursive,” “AI,” or “drift.”
