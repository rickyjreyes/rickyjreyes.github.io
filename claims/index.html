How to read status labels

Frozen holdout

Selection or model parameters were fixed before the holdout statistic was evaluated. This is stronger than a discovery scan, but still only tests the declared null/model.

Conditional

A signal or derivation exists, but a material dependency—such as background choice, calibration, transfer validity, or an unresolved assumption—changes the strength of the conclusion.

Route closed

A predeclared analysis path failed its own adequacy gate. The failure is preserved rather than tuned away, and it does not get rewritten as an unperformed target test.

Open-data numerical support

The result is reproducible against public data in the author-run pipeline, but independent replication and interpretation remain separate evidence layers.

Theory / empirically open

The claim is mathematically or conceptually specified but does not yet have claim-level empirical evidence sufficient to state it as an observed physical fact.

Independent replication

Reserved for evidence generated outside the author-controlled analysis path with a sufficiently independent dataset, implementation, or research group. None of the rows above is silently upgraded to that status.
