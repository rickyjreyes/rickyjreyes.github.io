01 / Nonlinear PDE

Binding, for every permitted split.

I(m) < I(μ) + I(m − μ),   0 < μ < m

Strict subadditivity of the constrained energy for every nontrivial split in a stated sufficient mass interval. Two-dimensional cubic–quintic model with β, γ > 0 and 0 < m ≤ mB, not an all-mass theorem.

02 / Nonlinear PDE

Existence without imposed walls.

I(m) is attained by a constrained minimizer

The constrained energy infimum is attained in the specified two-dimensional sufficient mass range. Whole-space minimizer set with compactness modulo translations, not profile uniqueness.

03 / Nonlinear PDE

Stability of an entire minimizing set.

distH²(u(t), Gm) remains small

A solution that begins near the constrained minimizers remains near their complete orbit in the specified H² setting. Set stability in the sufficient mass range, not uniqueness or stability of every excited state.

05 / Geometry

A precise geometric obstruction.

rank(g) ≤ 2 on a four-dimensional tangent space

The real-part scalar-gradient Gram tensor of a complex scalar field has restricted rank and a degenerate positive-semidefinite signature in four dimensions. Applies to this specific tensor construction, not all scalar-derived effective metrics.
