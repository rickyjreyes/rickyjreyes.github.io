Selected papers and theorem sources

Results you can inspect.

Each entry leads to an analytical manuscript and supporting formal source. A verified Lean declaration is narrower than a claim of general physical validation.

F01 / Representative model

i∂ₜu = (Δ + k²)²u − β|u|²u + γ|u|⁴u

Fourth-order cubic-quintic Schrödinger evolution on ℝ² with β, γ > 0. Existence, binding and stability statements depend on explicit coefficient and mass restrictions.

F01 / Variational PDE

Strict binding

A strict subadditivity theorem for the constrained fourth-order cubic-quintic Hamiltonian in an explicit sufficient mass range. It does not cover arbitrary masses.

Manuscript PDFLean source

F01 / Compactness

Whole-space minimizers

Existence and compactness of constrained minimizers under specified coefficients, dimension and mass assumptions, not a universal confinement theorem.

Manuscript PDFLean source

F01 / Evolution

Orbital stability

Orbital stability of the complete minimizing set, rather than stability of every individual wave profile or asymptotic attraction.

Manuscript PDFLean source

F08 / Geometric obstruction

Scalar-gradient metric rank

The real-part scalar-gradient Gram construction is positive semidefinite and rank at most two. This excludes a Lorentzian metric from that construction alone.

Manuscript PDFLean source

F09 / Inverse problems

Local identifiability

Structural nullity constraints on a specified 11-by-31 sensitivity map. The result is local, not a general global inverse theorem.

Manuscript PDFLean source

F10 / Spectral analysis

Fourier-shell exclusion

A nonzero L² field cannot have Fourier support restricted to a measure-zero isolated shell; assumptions matter for physical interpretations.

Manuscript PDFLean source
