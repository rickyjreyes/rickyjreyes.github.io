03 · Detector prediction and test record

Detector-facing predictions, frozen tests, and cross-domain checks

This branch separates prospective detector targets from retrospective discovery scans. JUNO defines a detector-response constraint for a ghost-mode modulation; LHC/LHCb defines collider log-periodic phenomenology; CMS evaluates a frozen waveform on an untouched target; GWTC separates training selection from holdout evaluation; and NIST tests frequency stability across atomic-data binning choices.

01 · prediction targets0%

The detector record begins with pre-test targets: JUNO constrains resolution-surviving ghost-mode modulation, while LHC/LHCb specifies log-periodic collider structure.

01 · prediction map

Detector hypotheses are separated from subsequent test statistics.

The detector-facing record contains two pre-test branches. JUNO specifies survival of a WCT-motivated log-energy / ghost-mode modulation under realistic detector response. LHC/LHCb specifies a log-periodic collider deformation and its corresponding open-data spectral search.

02 · JUNO · prospective

JUNO defines a prospective detector-response criterion.

The November 20, 2025 study derives conditions under which a WCT-motivated modulation could remain visible after JUNO resolution, smearing, and systematic limitations. Its value here is prospective: it defines a detector-facing falsification target rather than claiming a JUNO detection.

04 · CMS · frozen waveform

CMS evaluates pre-frozen waveform parameters on the G2 target.

Before the G2 target was inspected, frequency, phase, sign, selection rules, binning, masks, background treatment, null sizes, and random seed were frozen. The holdout returned A=0.9708618 and Δχ²=126.2832 under that fixed background; alternative smooth backgrounds materially weaken the apparent significance.

05 · GWTC · frozen holdout

GWTC separates frequency selection from holdout evaluation.

Training-only selection fixed k*=9.6023256 using 173 events. The 104-event holdout returned ΔD=10.0354 and Monte Carlo p=0.00129987 under the declared null. A KDE formulation is supporting analysis on the same already-inspected holdout, not a second independent replication.

06 · NIST · atomic open data

NIST tests frequency-selection stability across three binning schemes.

Across 120, 160, and 200 bins, the Fe II analysis selected the same k=31.3265306122449, with 0/5000 null exceedances at each setting. It is a separate public-data domain, but remains an author-run analysis rather than independent replication.

07 · unresolved

The next discriminator is independent replication plus causal identification.

The evidence story now contains prospective detector targets, frozen holdouts, conditional collider evidence, and cross-binning atomic support. The strongest next discriminator is independent replication on new data or a genuinely independent implementation, together with a physical-cause test that separates the proposed mechanism from background and reconstruction effects.
