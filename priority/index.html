Audit rule

Priority is not the same claim as influence.

A later paper can independently converge on a mechanism that appeared earlier in WCT. The registry therefore records chronology and structural correspondence while separately tracking older prior art.

Priority

A sufficiently specific public WCT statement exists before the comparison work.

Convergence

A later independent work exhibits the same or a closely corresponding mechanism.

Influence

Evidence that later authors encountered the earlier WCT work. Similarity alone does not establish this.

Prior-art gate

Older literature on generic ingredients is retained explicitly so WCT is not credited with established pre-WCT mechanisms.

Negative controls

Older prior art is part of the record.

The historical claim is not that WCT invented every ingredient. Several important primitives substantially predate the WCT corpus; they therefore function as controls on what can legitimately be claimed as original.

Localized Swift-Hohenberg states / homoclinic snaking

Finite-wavenumber localization and related pattern-selection mechanisms predate WCT. WCT priority claims must therefore concern narrower combinations, not Swift-Hohenberg localization itself.

Toroidal BEC persistent currents

Toroidal confinement, winding, and persistent circulation were studied well before 2025. The narrower WCT comparison concerns how topology is combined with curvature feedback, spectral selection, and mass/locking constructions.

Pure-quartic solitons

Fourth-order-dispersion solitons were experimentally demonstrated before WCT. Later high-order-dispersion results are therefore convergence/context unless the compared architecture contains additional distinctive WCT structure.
