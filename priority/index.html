Audit rule

Priority, origin, influence, and patent scope are separate questions.

The registry records what dated public records establish without presuming copying or independence. Later technical overlap can be documented while the pathway of influence remains unresolved.

Scholarly / technical priority

A specific technical architecture, mechanism, terminology, prediction, or construction appears in a verifiable Reyes public record before the relevant external work.

Documented origin

The earliest currently identified public record in this registry containing the listed contribution.

Patent-family priority

The earliest reported priority date for a filed application family. It establishes filing chronology, not patentability, claim scope, validity, enforceability, or infringement.

Structural convergence

A later external work exhibits the same or a closely corresponding mechanism or architecture.

Influence / derivation

Chronology and structural similarity alone do not establish derivation. Evidence may include access, distinctive terminology, uncommon mathematical constructions, matching parameters or figures, code similarity, citation trails, communications, or other provenance evidence.

Negative controls

Older prior art is part of the record.

The historical claim is not that WCT invented every ingredient. Important primitives substantially predate the WCT corpus and constrain what can legitimately be claimed as original.

Localized Swift-Hohenberg states / homoclinic snaking

Finite-wavenumber localization and related pattern-selection mechanisms predate WCT. WCT priority claims must concern narrower combinations, not Swift-Hohenberg localization itself.

Toroidal BEC persistent currents

Toroidal confinement, winding, and persistent circulation were studied well before 2025. The narrower WCT comparison concerns how topology is combined with curvature feedback, spectral selection, and mass/locking constructions.

Pure-quartic solitons

Fourth-order-dispersion solitons were experimentally demonstrated before WCT. Later high-order-dispersion results are convergence/context unless the compared architecture contains additional distinctive WCT structure.
