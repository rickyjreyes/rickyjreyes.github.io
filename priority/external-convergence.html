Pattern across the body

The signal is architectural, not a single shared ingredient.

Confinement → localization

Self-bound droplets, solitons, ring states, and nonlinear eigenmodes provide comparison points for the WCT question of whether finite-energy localized states can form and persist.

Finite-k → stabilization

k-gap, Swift-Hohenberg, high-order-dispersion, time-crystal, and spectral-selection work is most relevant where a preferred nonzero wavenumber is selected and nonlinear dynamics arrests growth into a persistent state.

Topology → winding → gauge structure

Toroidal condensates, persistent-current sectors, vortices, Hall-torus systems, and density-dependent gauge fields are highest-value comparisons when these mechanisms occur together rather than as isolated established ingredients.

Promotion standard

What would move a candidate into the main provenance registry?

A candidate should be promoted only after: (1) exact WCT anchor and public date are identified; (2) the external manuscript/publication chronology is normalized; (3) older prior art is checked so only the narrower WCT combination is compared; and (4) mechanism-level correspondence is written without assuming influence. Evidence of derivation requires a separate provenance layer such as documented access, distinctive equation-level correspondence, matching unusual parameters or figures, terminology transfer, citation trails, communications, or code similarity.
