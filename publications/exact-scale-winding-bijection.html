Abstract

This work formalizes an exact mapping between discrete scale changes in logarithmic coordinates and integer phase winding in the WCT active-domain construction, providing a mathematical bridge between log-periodicity and phase closure.

Research question

Can discrete scale invariance be mapped exactly to WCT active-domain phase winding rather than treated only as a phenomenological analogy?

  • Connects discrete scale invariance to active-domain phase closure within the WCT mathematical framework.
