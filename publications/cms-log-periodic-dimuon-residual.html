Abstract

CMS Run-2 dimuon residual: a frozen log-frequency selected in Run2016H reproduced across independent-file and cross-period tests; a prospective Run2016G G2 phase-locked holdout returned positive amplitude A=0.9708618 and Δχ²=126.2832 under the declared background procedure. An independent adversarial audit reproduced the locked score but found 359–442 exceedances per 1,000 trials under defensible alternative smooth-background generators. Recurrence under the historical pipeline is reproducible; model-robust discovery significance and physical attribution remain unresolved.

Plain-language overview

Research question

Does a fixed log-periodic dimuon residual recur across independent CMS Run-2 files and periods when frequency, phase, and sign are progressively frozen?

Main contribution

  • Documents frozen-frequency independent-file and cross-period recurrence and the prospective G2 fixed-frequency, fixed-phase, positive-amplitude holdout (A=0.9708618; Δχ²=126.2832).
  • Preserves the independent adversarial audit: alternative smooth-null generators yielded 359/1000 (degree-12 log-polynomial), 405/1000 (penalized spline), and 442/1000 (spline s=1) exceedances.
  • Distinguishes conditional historical significance from model-robust significance, detector/systematic alternatives, and WCT attribution.

Evidence type

Public-data analysisProspective holdoutAdversarial background audit

Current limitations

The recurring residual is reproducible under the declared pipeline, but several defensible smooth-background models generate comparable scores in 359–442 of 1,000 trials. Conversely, flexible backgrounds can also absorb injected signals; neither disappearance nor recurrence establishes the physical cause. No validated model-robust global p-value or WCT attribution is claimed.
