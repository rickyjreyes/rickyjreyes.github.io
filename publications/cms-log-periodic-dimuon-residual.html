Abstract

This CMS Run-2 open-data study follows a fixed-frequency dimuon residual through independent-file replication, cross-period replication, and a prospective phase-locked holdout, then audits the result against flexible smooth-background alternatives.

Plain-language overview

Research question

Does a fixed log-periodic dimuon residual recur across independent CMS Run-2 files and periods when frequency, phase, and sign are progressively frozen?

Main contribution

  • Reports independent-file and cross-period recurrence of a frozen dimuon log-frequency.
  • Tests a prospectively phase-locked holdout and documents the later background-identifiability audit.

Evidence type

Public-data analysisProspective holdoutAdversarial background audit

Current limitations

The recurring residual is reproducible under the declared pipeline, but model-robust significance and physical attribution remain unresolved because reasonable smooth backgrounds can generate comparable scores.
