Abstract

This open-data study tests a WCT-motivated log-periodic mode in GWTC chirp masses using a frozen holdout design, unbinned robustness checks, structured non-periodic population nulls, and a prospective prediction for future catalogs.

Plain-language overview

Research question

Does a frequency selected on training data recur in held-out GWTC chirp-mass data and survive stronger non-periodic population nulls?

Main contribution

  • Freezes a chirp-mass log-frequency before holdout evaluation and reports the resulting holdout statistic.
  • Adds unbinned robustness and structured non-periodic population-null tests while preserving a future-catalog prediction.

Evidence type

Public-data analysisFrozen holdoutStructured-null robustness

Current limitations

The result remains author-run and does not replace LVK hierarchical population inference with posterior propagation and injection-calibrated selection effects.
