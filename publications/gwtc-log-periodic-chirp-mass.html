Abstract

GWTC chirp-mass analysis: training N=173 selected k=9.602325620315224; a frozen 104-event holdout returned Δ2lnL=10.035425 and Monte Carlo p=0.00129987 under its declared smooth null. An unbinned training-only KDE robustness check returned p=0.00089991 on the same already-inspected holdout. V4 fixed k=9.7, amplitude, and phase before a structured non-periodic population-null evaluation; empirical p-values were 0.00559944, 0.00019998, and 0.00029997 for selection exponents γ=0, 1.5, and 2.5. These are conditional tests, not independent future-catalog replication or WCT physical attribution.

Plain-language overview

Research question

Does a frequency selected on training data recur in held-out GWTC chirp-mass data and survive stronger non-periodic population nulls?

Main contribution

  • Reports training N=173, frozen holdout N=104, Δ2lnL=10.035425 and empirical p=0.00129987 under the V1 specified null.
  • Separately documents V3 unbinned KDE robustness (p=0.00089991 on the already-inspected holdout) and V4 frozen structured non-periodic population-null challenge (worst-case empirical p=0.00559944).
  • Preserves a genuinely unseen future-catalog prediction as a distinct pending test.

Evidence type

Current limitations

All tests are author-run. V3 and V4 reuse the GWTC-5 holdout after prior inspection and are supporting robustness analyses, not additional independent holdouts. V4 uses a phenomenological structured population and approximate selection weights rather than event-posteriors and injection-calibrated LVK hierarchical selection. A genuinely future-catalog test and physical-cause discrimination remain open.
