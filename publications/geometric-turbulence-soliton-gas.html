Abstract

This numerical study examines phase-locked curvature filaments in driven nonlinear WCT-like dynamics and reports a soliton-gas regime with persistent localized structures and a characteristic filament-length distribution.

Plain-language overview

Research question

Do driven nonlinear curvature dynamics self-organize into persistent phase-locked filaments with statistically stable length structure?

Main contribution

  • Simulates phase-locked curvature-filament formation in three-dimensional nonlinear dynamics.
  • Compares filament-length statistics and reports a persistent soliton-gas regime.

Evidence type

Numerical simulationStatistical model comparison

Current limitations

The result is numerical and model-specific; continuum existence, unbounded-domain stability, and direct physical correspondence remain open.
