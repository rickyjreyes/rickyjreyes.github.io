Abstract

This work extends the RCA program with a propagation-versus-correction criticality law for recursive AI systems and audits a prospective quantitative prediction against a later observed incident.

Plain-language overview

Research question

Can recursive AI instability be characterized by a propagation-versus-correction criticality variable that supports prospective quantitative prediction?

Main contribution

  • Defines a propagation-correction criticality law for recursive AI dynamics.
  • Connects the model to a prospectively stated quantitative prediction and subsequent validation audit.

Evidence type

Mathematical derivationProspective prediction audit

Current limitations

The current evidence is limited to a small number of author-scored cases; broader preregistered benchmarks and independent evaluation are needed.
