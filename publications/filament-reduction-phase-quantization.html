Abstract

This work reduces curvature-locked field structure to an effective filament description and derives phase-quantization conditions for localized modes within the WCT spectral-dynamics program.

Plain-language overview

Research question

Can localized curvature-locked field configurations be reduced to filament dynamics with explicit phase-quantization conditions?

Main contribution

  • Develops an effective filament reduction for curvature-locked modes.
  • Derives phase-closure and quantization relations for the reduced dynamics.

Evidence type

Mathematical derivation

Current limitations

The reduced model still requires continuum-level existence and stability results and empirical identification with physical localized structures.
