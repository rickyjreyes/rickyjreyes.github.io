Standard of contribution

Make the result independently checkable.

The goal is not to collect unsupported claims. The goal is to make mathematical statements precise enough to prove, refute or situate in the literature.

Meaningful contributions include exact proofs, rigorous no-go results, parameter-specific classifications, Lean 4 formalizations, corrected hypotheses, prior-art identifications and counterexamples.

  • Specify the model, domain, function spaces, parameters and quantifiers.
  • Show the mathematical argument or actual checked source declaration.
  • Compare against the nearest existing theorem and identify what differs.
  • Record which part of the GC question is answered and what remains.
  • Include reproducibility evidence when claiming a formal or numerical verification.

A theorem establishing one sufficient mass range does not automatically settle all masses. A proof in Lean establishes the encoded mathematical statement, not the physical interpretation of its assumptions.
