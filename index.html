Current evidence snapshot · October 6, 2026

What the program currently shows—and what it does not.

This is the shortest status view for readers who want the empirical result before the broader theory. Statistical statements below apply to their stated analysis and null/background model; they are not presented as validation of every WCT claim.

01GWTC frozen holdout

Training selected k=9.6023256; the 104-event holdout returned ΔD=10.0354 with Monte Carlo p=0.00129987 under the frozen test. This is the strongest clean frozen holdout currently listed.

02CMS fixed-waveform residual

The prospective G2 test returned A=0.9708618 and Δχ²=126.2832 under the declared background. The effect is background-model sensitive; alternative smooth backgrounds reduce it substantially.

03ATLAS transfer test

No positive cross-detector replication was obtained. V6.1 closed as V6_FAILED_HIERARCHICAL_ROUTE_CLOSED with no qualifying nuisance dimension, before the final CMS-frequency target was evaluated.

04Core emergence claims

Claims that mass, force, or effective spacetime emerge from confinement remain theory-level proposals unless a claim-specific ledger entry states a stronger empirical status.

The research idea

Persistent structure as confined wave dynamics.

Wave Confinement Theory is a proposed geometric wave framework in which localized physical structure arises from sustained oscillation, finite-wavenumber selection, phase organization, curvature feedback, and topology.

01Wave transport

Energy and phase propagate through a field.

02Finite-band selection

A preferred spectral shell suppresses unrestricted growth.

03Resonant confinement

Boundary and feedback conditions organize persistent modes.

04Curvature locking

Phase, geometry, and topology stabilize localized structure.

Canonical equation build · E1B → E5 → E6

From loop geometry to the mass-curvature relation.

The diagram reveals the registered chain progressively while the canonical equations remain static, selectable, and linked to the equation registry.

01 · E1B · geometry

Combine curvature and torsion into a spectral rate.

σ(s) = √(κ(s)² + τ(s)²)

E1B registers σ as an inverse-length curvature spectral rate. This is a geometric definition, not a particle-mass measurement.

02 · E5 · loop closure

Relate winding to the loop-averaged curvature rate.

kwind = kσ = ⟨σ⟩w

The registered E5 chain is model-conditional on compatible orientation, exact integrated locking, and a positive constant-weight condition.

03 · E6 · dimensional map

Map the effective inverse-length scale to rest mass.

m = (ℏ / c) keff

E6 passes dimensional closure. That status does not establish that WCT dynamics generates observed particle masses.

04 · composed relation

Under the E5 assumptions, substitute the curvature average.

m = (ℏ / c) ⟨σ⟩w
