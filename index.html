Frozen holdout · GWTC

GWTC chirp-mass mode survives the frozen holdout

Training-only selection froze k*=9.6023256 before the GWTC-5 holdout evaluation. The holdout returned ΔD=10.035425 with p=0.00129987. A separately formulated KDE analysis selected k*=9.7941176 and returned p=0.00089991 on the same holdout as a supporting analysis.

Prospective phase-locked holdout · CMS

Frozen CMS waveform persists across file and period tests

After discovery, the CMS frequency was frozen rather than rescanned. An unused Run2016H file reproduced it with Ar=0.9367 and Δχ²=118.91, followed by a preregistered Run2016G test with Ar=0.9349 and Δχ²=115.89. The later untouched G2 phase-locked holdout returned A=0.9708618 and Δχ²=126.2832. Significance remains background-model dependent.

The research idea

Persistent structure as confined wave dynamics.

Wave Confinement Theory is a proposed geometric wave framework in which localized physical structure arises from sustained oscillation, finite-wavenumber selection, phase organization, curvature feedback, and topology.

Equation build-up · registered chain

From a closed curve to an inverse-length scale.

Curvature and torsion of a closed curve Γ combine into a spectral rate; its weighted average sets the effective wavenumber; under the stated E5 conditions, that maps dimensionally to a mass scale. Assumptions, status, Lean coverage, SymPy checks, and limitations are kept in the equation registry.

About

Engineering practice and independent research.

Richard J. Reyes is a controls engineer and software developer working in industrial refrigeration and process automation. His engineering work includes PLC logic, instrumentation, alarms, sequencing, networked HMIs, commissioning, and control-system troubleshooting.

He holds a B.S. in Computer Science from San José State University. His independent research spans nonlinear wave dynamics, mathematical physics, scientific computing, experimental signal analysis, public-dataset studies, computational architecture, and resonance-based control.
