Prior literature · publication cutoff 2024

Pre-2025 Scientific Foundations

A relevance-ranked map of established literature that bears most directly on the mathematical and physical questions addressed by Wave Confinement Theory: localized nonlinear states, finite-band mode selection, existence and stability, closed-curve geometry, phase and flux, optical resonance, mass structure, and empirical particle-physics comparisons.

Interpretation boundary. Every item on this page was published before 2025. Inclusion records technical relevance or prior scientific context only. It does not imply that the cited authors anticipated WCT as a whole, that WCT derives from any one source, or that these works independently validate WCT. The separate Overlap Ledger is reserved for post-date comparison records.
