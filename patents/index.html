Research comparison · reviewed November 16, 2026

Reyes first. DARPA's later public announcements. Side by side.

These eight comparisons start with a dated Reyes 2025 filing or author-dated research record and contrast it with a 2026 DARPA public notice, solicitation or award listing. The purpose is to show what Reyes had already described, what DARPA later publicly sought, and what remains materially different. The sequence establishes chronology of the cited records, not authorship of DARPA's concepts or independent patent-claim priority.

How to read this section

Each entry states the earliest available Reyes paper or application-family record, the DARPA program and its public date, the precise shared engineering objective, and the unmatched or undisclosed implementation details. 2026 awardee-list entries have year-only public dates and are not described as inventions completed in 2026. Original 2025 dates of papers are as recorded in the author's research chronology; official patent priority for particular subject matter requires examination of the filed disclosure.

01

DICE · decentralized AI coordination and inference control

April 21, 2026
Reyes — earlier dated work

Reyes, 2025: Resonance-Confinement Architecture (June 11, 2025 author chronology); WaveLock/symbolic-memory provisional filed June 18, 2025. Addresses bounded AI behavior, drift and coherence-regulating feedback.

DARPA — later public record

DARPA, April 21, 2026: DICE, a program to coordinate decentralized AI teams through local inference control, role coherence and long-horizon alignment. Dated notice.

Where they overlap: Sustained coherent behavior and control of AI-agent systems across repeated inference and coordination steps.

Where they differ / what is not established: DICE describes distributed consensus, peer-to-peer teaming and inference-time control. Its public description does not implement or cite a Reyes curvature-based coherence governor or WaveLock commitments.

Specific shared safety objective · no verified implementation equivalence
02

Paleja · formal methods for emergent AI-team failure

October 12, 2026
Reyes — earlier dated work

Reyes, 2025: RCA paper and WaveLock/symbolic-memory provisional (June 18, 2025) describe monitoring and constraining coherence failures in computational-agent systems.

DARPA — later public record

2026 DARPA Young Faculty Award: Rohan Paleja, Purdue, proposes formal methods to prevent, detect and contain collective failure, deception and collusion in AI teams.

Where they overlap: Unexpected collective behavior and the need to prevent or detect unsafe multi-agent deviations.

Where they differ / what is not established: Paleja's stated aim is formal guarantees about interacting agents. The public announcement does not specify a curvature-state detector, statistical drift ratchet or replay-verifiable commitment architecture.

Shared failure-prevention objective · no verified implementation equivalence
03

AMCOM-DI · self-monitoring reasoning and auditable adaptation

November 3, 2026
Reyes — earlier dated work

Reyes, 2025: WaveLock/symbolic-memory provisional (June 18, 2025) and RCA; later nonprovisional filed June 7, 2026. Describes coherence-state monitoring, drift decisions and auditable state-linked records.

DARPA — later public record

DARPA, November 3, 2026: AMCOM-DI solicits a structured metacognitive engine that measures reasoning drift, representational coherence and uncertainty, locally adapts, and emits reproducible reasoning traces.

Where they overlap: Internal-state coherence assessment, drift detection, bounded corrective behavior and reconstructable audit evidence.

Where they differ / what is not established: AMCOM-DI focuses on heterogeneous intelligence collection and cost-aware planning. No matching curvature-PDE evolution, calibration/invariant combination, ratchet threshold or dual-hash attestation is identified in the solicitation.

Specific shared monitoring objective · no verified implementation equivalence

What this comparison does not claim

The cited sources do not establish that DARPA or its performers accessed Reyes's work, reproduced a distinctive Reyes mechanism, adopted WCT, or infringed any patent. The strongest shared public objectives concern AI drift and coherence (DICE, AMCOM-DI), structured symbolic state (HYPER-CAP), and physical memory (Crane). Other 2026 awards involving underwater optics, quantum fields or general agentic information theory were excluded because a specific technical correspondence was not established. Unpublished claim sets and implementation parameters remain off this public site.

Evidence boundaries

What the public labels mean.

The portfolio keeps legal status, theoretical work, implementation, experiments, and independent validation separate.

Public code

A repository or executable implementation is available for inspection. Security, correctness, performance, and product readiness are evaluated as separate dimensions.

Author experiment

A measurement or observation was conducted and reported by the inventor. Replication source and status are recorded separately.

Theory proposal

A mathematical or physical framework has been publicly described. Empirical tests and validation status are recorded separately.

Architecture concept

A system design or embodiment has been described at a public, non-proprietary level. Implementation status is recorded separately for each family.
