WCT Post-Date Overlap Ledger

This ledger records external papers, preprints, datasets, and experimental results identified after the corresponding Richard J. Reyes / Wave Confinement Theory research disclosures and ranks the degree of technical correspondence. The purpose is to preserve a public, timestamped record of later convergence around structures, mechanisms, and architectures already present in the Reyes research program.

Post-Date Overlap Score.The 0–10 score ranks the density and specificity of technical overlap after date ordering: higher values indicate stronger multi-feature correspondence with earlier Reyes/WCT work. It is a comparison score, not a probability of influence, copying, derivation, misconduct, or infringement.
Chronology anchors.The separate Priority & Provenance Registry contains exact DOI-backed Reyes disclosure dates and the strongest date-normalized comparison cases. Rows marked DATE-CHECKED have already had the relevant WCT anchor date and external publication chronology normalized there.
Bibliographic normalization.Paper titles, author lists, and DOI/permanent identifiers are normalized to the linked primary record. arXiv entries show their DataCite DOI in the identifier column. Where a source has no DOI, the ledger says so and links to the primary record instead of inventing an identifier.
