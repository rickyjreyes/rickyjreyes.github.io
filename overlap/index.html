04 · EXACT IDENTITY E57 · Biharmonic expansion and Fourier symbol \( \displaystyle (\Delta+k_*^{2})^{2}=\Delta^{2}+2k_*^{2}\Delta+k_*^{4} \) +

On Schwartz functions, E57 formalizes the differential-operator identity and Fourier multiplier:

\( \displaystyle \widehat{\mathcal{A}u}(\xi)=(|\xi|^{2}-k_*^{2})^{2}\widehat{u}(\xi) \)

Its operator is also proved nonnegative and self-adjoint, with a continuum shell-damping semigroup. These statements concern E57, not the paper's complete driven Kerr system.

05 · RIGOROUS SPECTRUM E61 · Spectrum and semigroup stability threshold \( \displaystyle \sigma(L_r)=(-\infty,r],\quad \|e^{tL_r}\|=e^{rt} \) +

E61 proves the exact spectrum and operator norm for the related linear operator:

\( \displaystyle L_r=rI-a(\Delta+k_*^{2})^{2},\qquad a>0,\quad k_*>0 \)

On \(L^{2}(\mathbb{R}^{d})\), for \(d\geq1\) and \(t\geq0\), the linear threshold is \(r=0\). Negative \(r\) yields exponential decay; positive \(r\) admits growing solutions. This is not an orbital-stability theorem for the driven nonlinear optical cavity.

06 · NUMERICAL RESULT Aberration-dependent localized intensity \( \displaystyle \Delta I \propto |S|^{1/4} \) 2D numerical scan +

In Fleck et al., Section III and Figure 7, \(\Delta I\) denotes localized excess intensity above the continuous-wave background. The numerically observed intensity scales as \(|S|^{1/4}\) and vanishes as the fourth-order aberration tends to zero.

This is a two-dimensional numerical scaling result, not an analytic theorem or a measured three-dimensional exponent.
